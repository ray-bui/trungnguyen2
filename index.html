From Kiln to Site

Five stages. Every batch traceable.

Nothing ships until it has passed through all five — the same sequence, the same checks, whether the order is 500 or 500,000 square meters.

01

Raw Material Blending

Kaolin, feldspar, and clay milled and blended to a precise formula for consistent body density.

02

Pressing & Shaping

Hydraulic presses form each tile under high pressure for dimensional accuracy and strength.

03

Glazing & Digital Printing

Multi-layer glaze and high-resolution digital printing give each surface its final finish.

04

Firing

Fired at controlled kiln temperatures for structural density and long-term durability.

1200°C
05

Quality Control & Packaging

Every batch tested for warpage and water absorption, then crated for export-grade transit.
